Route apps don’t know you’re towing.

Google Maps and Apple Maps have no passenger-car trailer mode. Road signs and posted restrictions always win. Avoid tight lots, low clearances, sudden reroutes, and any road that looks unsuitable. Pull over safely before changing navigation.
